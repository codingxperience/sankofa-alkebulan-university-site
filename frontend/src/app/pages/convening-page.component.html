Convening 2026 Background

Sankofa Alkebulan University is founded on the Akan principle of Sankofa — to go back and retrieve what has been lost — as a method for building a stronger and more enlightened future. The Sankofa Convening is where the university first opens that work to the public. In 2026 it launches two flagship programmes: the Chancellor’s founding keynote, which sets out the university’s vision, mission and academic philosophy and its strategic role in advancing African civilisational studies; and a mini-conference on practical pathways for strengthening cultural identity, indigenous knowledge and intellectual cooperation — across institutions, borders and generations. The Convening is free and open to all, at Speke Resort, Munyonyo, and online.
