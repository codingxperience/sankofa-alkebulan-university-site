Field dispatch

Convention week, May 20-25, Kampala.

A visual and editorial record of arrival diplomacy, diaspora economic dialogue, cultural diplomacy, the SAU publication table, and Liberation Day movement.

Dispatch 25 May 2026 Author · University Press 9 min read

SAU press desk archives Pan-African convention week in Kampala.

Kampala's convention week placed African public memory, diaspora capital, cultural diplomacy, tourism, and scholarly publication in the same civic field. The University Press records that field as an institutional archive of who gathered, what themes surfaced, and how knowledge moved through the week.

  • The archive follows the May 20-25 window through diplomacy, arrival, diaspora economic dialogue, public procession, and publication display.
  • POATE 2026 is treated as a parallel public-scholarship case: tourism, culture, enterprise, and destination leadership shape continental development.
  • Photographs, films, and scholarship sit inside one coherent institutional record.
